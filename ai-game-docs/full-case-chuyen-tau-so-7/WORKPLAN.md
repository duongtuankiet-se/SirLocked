## Narrative contract

The original bridge inspection dossier is diverted before train 7 departs at 00:15. Deputy Trần Tường Vy wants to conceal supplier commissions. Clock technician Quốc An's separate repair caused a twelve-minute offset and a misleading alibi. The player must separate that misconduct from the dossier theft.

Act I: hall and dispatch office establish the clock error and internal transfer. A mandatory paired confrontation and deduction open Act II.

Act II: luggage warehouse and signal cabin establish the actual route, the false cancellation telegram, and the unrelated clock repair. Two confrontations and a deduction open the archive.

Act III: accounts archive and covered freight platform establish beneficiary, altered recipient, witnessed custody and signed diversion order. Recover the original dossier, confront the deputy, make the final deduction and jointly accuse with three distinct evidence links.

Every required puzzle/camera/use/combine has a unique reward required by progression. Readiness candidates cannot depend on future scenes. Late questions on repeated NPCs are clue-gated because manual contract 1 ignores availableSceneIds.
